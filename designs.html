How did I get into Design?

I never thought about being a designer until I had to change my major halfway through college from photography to digital media and had to take some graphic design courses. Some of those courses I had to take taught me how to design collages, spreads, posters, logos, e-editions and motion graphics. I've made some cool stuff with the knowledge I have now and it broadens my skills to other things besides still photography. Thank you to my advisors for helping me switch majors, though I was skeptical, it was a great decision.
